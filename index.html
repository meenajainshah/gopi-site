How Our Running & Wellness Coaching Works

Whether you work with a running coach online or train with me at the Riverfront, coaching follows the same four steps.

  1. Initial Assessment

    We talk through your goals, running and fitness history, schedule, injuries and current habits.

  2. Personalised Training Plan

    You get a plan built for your level and goal, with runs, strength, mobility and recovery, plus nutrition guidance if you want it.

  3. Progress Tracking

    Your runs and training data are reviewed regularly, with feedback on pace, effort and form.

  4. Continuous Guidance

    The plan is adjusted as you improve, with ongoing support right up to race day and beyond.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can a running coach in Ahmedabad help me achieve my fitness and running goals?

A running coach gives you a plan matched to your current fitness and goal, reviews your runs, corrects your form and adjusts training as you improve, so you progress steadily and reduce the risk of doing too much too soon. In Ahmedabad you can train in supervised sessions at Riverfront or follow an online plan.

Can beginners join running coaching without any previous experience?

Yes. Beginners are welcome. You can start with the free 12-week beginner-to-10K plan, which begins with short run-walk intervals, or join personalised coaching that starts from your current fitness.

Do you offer online running and fitness coaching outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Online running coaching is available for runners anywhere, with personalised weekly plans, run-data review, video-based gait and posture review, and strength and mobility guidance.

How can I prepare for my first 5K, 10K, Half Marathon or Full Marathon?

Build up gradually: run three or four times a week at an easy pace, increase your long run slowly, include strength and mobility work, and allow time for recovery. Longer races need more weeks of preparation and practice with fuelling. A coach can set the right timeline for your race.

Do you provide personalised running training plans based on my fitness level and goals?

Yes. Every coaching plan is built around your running history, current fitness, schedule and goal race, and it is adjusted as your training data and feedback come in.

How can nutrition coaching improve my running performance, stamina and recovery?

Eating and hydrating well around training helps you have energy for your runs, sustain longer efforts and recover between sessions. Nutrition coaching covers pre-run and post-run food choices, hydration, fuelling for endurance training and everyday habits.

How do I choose the right running and fitness coaching programme for my goals?

Start from your goal and your schedule. Beginners can start with the free 10K plan; runners with a race target usually choose online coaching or supervised Riverfront sessions; and nutrition coaching can be added for weight management or fuelling. If you are unsure, book a consultation and we will choose together.
