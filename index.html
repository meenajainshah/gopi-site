Questions

Before you start

I have never run. Where do I start?

Yes. Start with my free 12 week plan. It mixes walking and running and builds you up to your first 10 km on your own. When you want feedback and accountability, the coached programmes begin at the next step.

How do I know which programme is right for me?

Tell me where you are with running and what you want to finish. I will reply on WhatsApp and suggest the right starting point.

What is a pacer?

A pacer runs a race at one steady speed to a target finish time, so other runners can follow. I have been the 2 hour 30 minute pacer at half marathons 20 times.

What pace is a 2 hour 30 minute half marathon?

A half marathon in 2 hours 30 minutes is about 7:07 per kilometre, or about 11:27 per mile, held for the full 21.1 km.

Where do the in-person sessions happen?

At the Riverfront in Ahmedabad, usually on Tuesday and Thursday mornings from 6:00 to 7:15 AM, with eight sessions a month.

Can I train with you if I do not live in Ahmedabad?

Yes. Online coaching gives you a weekly plan, run data review, video analysis and ongoing support from anywhere.

How long is the minimum commitment?

Three months. Longer commitments of six or twelve months bring the monthly fee down.

Do you offer nutrition coaching?

Yes. I am a Certified Nutrition and Diet Coach, and the nutrition plan is an add-on to your running programme.
