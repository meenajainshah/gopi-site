Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can a complete beginner use the free plan?

Yes. The free 12-week beginner-to-10K plan is a general starting resource for people building towards their first 10K. Follow it progressively and adapt or stop if you experience unusual pain or symptoms.

Do you coach runners outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Online coaching is available for runners beyond Ahmedabad. Training plans and feedback are delivered remotely.

Do you coach Half Marathon and Full Marathon runners?

Yes. Coaching is available for 10K, Half Marathon, Full Marathon, and ultra-distance goals, subject to an assessment of the runner's experience, current fitness, and race timeline.

Is nutrition included?

Nutrition and diet coaching is available as an add-on. The scope and plan are discussed according to the runner's goals.
