Distances

Running Training Plans for Different Race Distances

A 5K plan and an ultra plan share the same principles, consistency, gradual progression and recovery, but the emphasis changes with the distance. Shorter races reward speed and pacing; longer races depend on endurance, fuelling and time on feet. Your running plans are built around the distance you are training for and how much running you already do.

DistanceTraining focus
5KRunning consistency, aerobic foundation, and building to continuous running

Process

How Does Our Running Coaching Programme Work?

Whether you train online or at the Riverfront, coaching follows the same simple cycle: understand where you are, plan, run, review and adjust.

  1. AssessWe discuss your running history, current fitness, injuries, schedule and goal race.
  2. PlanYou receive a personalised training plan with easy runs, workouts, long runs, strength and recovery.
  3. TrainYou run the plan, online from anywhere or in supervised sessions at Riverfront, Ahmedabad.
  4. ReviewYour run data and form are reviewed, with feedback on pace, effort and technique.
  5. AdjustThe plan is updated as you improve, until race day and beyond.

Can beginners start running with your free 12-week training plan?

Yes. The free 12-week beginner-to-10K plan is built for people starting from walk-run sessions and working towards their first 10K. It uses three sessions a week at an easy pace. It is a general self-guided resource, so adapt or stop if you experience unusual pain or symptoms, and seek professional advice if you have a medical condition or injury.

Do you offer online running coaching outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. Online coaching is available for runners anywhere. You receive personalised weekly training plans, run-data review, video-based gait and posture review, and ongoing feedback, all delivered remotely.

Do you provide personal running coaching in Ahmedabad?

Yes. Physical coaching takes place at Riverfront, Ahmedabad, as supervised group sessions: eight sessions a month, typically Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6:00 AM to 7:15 AM, with live technique correction and gait and posture feedback for each runner.

Do you offer training plans for 5K, 10K, Half Marathon, Full Marathon and Ultra races?

Yes. Coaching covers 5K, 10K, Half Marathon, Full Marathon, high-altitude and ultra-distance goals. The plan depends on an assessment of your running experience, current fitness and race timeline.

What is included in your personalised running coaching programme?

Personalised weekly training plans, run-data review and structured feedback, video-based gait and posture review, running-form, foot-landing and breathing guidance, strength, core and mobility workouts, and accountability with ongoing coaching support.

Is nutrition and diet coaching included in running coaching?
