Approach

A Practical Approach to Healthy Eating and Sustainable Nutrition

No single diet suits everyone. Food choices should take account of individual needs, preferences, culture, routine, activity, and health context. The aim is not a short-term restrictive diet; it is to help you develop realistic habits that you can maintain.

Healthy nutrition for weight loss, better energy or stronger training comes from small, repeatable choices: regular meals, sensible portions, enough protein, fruit and vegetables, and good hydration. Fitness nutrition coaching adds timing around training and recovery. Everything is adjusted to what you enjoy eating, so the plan fits your life instead of fighting it.

Medical conditions: where a medical condition or therapeutic diet is involved, work with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.

Yes. Diet guidance is built around your goals, daily routine, food preferences and activity level, and it is reviewed and adjusted as you make progress.

Yes. You do not need any prior knowledge of nutrition. Coaching starts with your current habits and builds small, practical changes you can keep.

Can I get online nutrition coaching?

Yes. Nutrition coaching is based on conversations, plan reviews and follow-ups, so it can be arranged online if you live outside Ahmedabad. Mention it when you enquire.

How much does nutrition coaching cost in Ahmedabad?

A standalone nutrition and diet plan costs ₹2,500 to ₹2,750 a month, depending on whether you commit for 12, 6 or 3 months. If you also take running coaching, nutrition is available as an add-on from ₹1,500 to ₹1,750 a month.

Can nutrition coaching help with weight management?

Yes. Diet coaching for weight loss focuses on practical food choices, portion awareness, habit-building and regular progress reviews rather than crash diets. Where a medical condition is involved, work with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.

Do you provide nutrition guidance for runners?

Yes. Fitness nutrition coaching for runners covers pre-run and post-run food choices, hydration, fuelling during endurance training and recovery-focused habits.
