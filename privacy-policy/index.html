Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026. This explains what personal details this website collects, why, and who else sees them.

Who is responsible

This website is run by Coach Gopi (Gopi D), a running coach, nutrition and diet coach, wellness consultant and running event consultant based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean Coach Gopi. You can reach me through the Contact page.

What I collect

I only collect what you choose to send me:

  • Enquiry forms (Contact, Corporate Wellness, Events): your name, email address or phone number, and whatever else you type, such as your company name, city, number of employees, event details or message.
  • WhatsApp: if you message me on WhatsApp, I see the number and the messages you send.
  • Health details: if you tell me about an injury or a medical condition so that I can guide you, I use it only for that purpose. Please share only what you are comfortable with.

The share card pages let you type a name to put on a card. That name appears only on the card and in the link you create. This website does not store it.

How enquiries reach me

This website has no database of its own. When you send an enquiry form, it is emailed to me through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service. FormSubmit handles the message on its way to my inbox. The enquiry then sits in my email like any other message.

Why I use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry and suggest the right coaching, nutrition, wellness or event support.
  • To provide a service you ask for, including plans, feedback and invoices.
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations, where they apply.

I do not sell your details and I do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

Who else may see your information

  • FormSubmit, which forwards form enquiries to my email.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you contact me there.
  • Instagram (Meta): the home page shows embedded Instagram posts. Loading them lets Instagram see that your browser visited the page.
  • Google Fonts: the site loads its fonts from Google, which receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • The website host (Render), which keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses and the pages requested.

These services have their own privacy policies. I do not control them.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not currently use advertising or analytics tracking. Embedded content such as Instagram posts may set cookies from the provider. If I add analytics later, I will update this page.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiry emails for as long as I need them to reply to you, to provide a service, and for any records the law requires. You can ask me to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you the details I hold about you, correct them, or delete them, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Contact me through the Contact page and I will respond as soon as I can. These choices are offered in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact me for you.

Security

I take reasonable care to protect the details you send me, but no method of sending information over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle personal details, I will update this page and the date at the top.
