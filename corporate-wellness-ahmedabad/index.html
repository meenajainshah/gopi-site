Corporate wellness

Corporate Wellness Programmes That Get Employees MovingHealthier employees. More connected teams. A more active workplace.

Employee wellness works best when it is practical, inclusive, engaging, and consistent. I work with SMEs and corporate teams to design group fitness and wellness challenges that encourage employees to move more, build healthier habits, and connect with colleagues.

Programmes can be tailored to company size, departments, work patterns, fitness levels, and engagement objectives. Activities can be delivered as monthly campaigns, short-term challenges, or a structured annual wellness calendar.

Who we work with

  • Small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs).
  • Corporate HR and People & Culture teams.
  • Business owners and founders.
  • Organisations looking to introduce or refresh employee wellness activities.
  • Teams seeking interdepartmental engagement through movement and fitness.

Programmes

Corporate wellness programmes

Steps

Corporate step challenge

Encourage employees to build daily movement habits through individual and team step goals, weekly leaderboards, team recognition, and monthly results.

Walk and run

Walking and running challenges

Offer inclusive walking and running activities for employees with different starting fitness levels. Options may include beginner walk-run participation, distance goals, group sessions, and preparation challenges.

Teams

Department-versus-department challenges

Create friendly competition between departments such as Sales, HR, Finance, Operations, and Technology. Track agreed team activities and celebrate participation, consistency, and collective progress.

Monthly

Monthly wellness challenges

Keep engagement fresh with themed campaigns such as a step challenge, active commute week, walking and running month, mobility challenge, or team endurance challenge.

Sessions

Group fitness and running sessions

Arrange guided group activities such as walking, running, beginner fitness, running technique, strength, and mobility sessions. Session design depends on the organisation's location, facilities, schedule, and participant needs.

How a programme works

  1. DiscoverDiscuss workforce size, objectives, work patterns, current activities, and practical constraints.
  2. DesignAgree the challenge format, duration, participation rules, measurement approach, and recognition plan.
  3. LaunchProvide clear communications and encourage employees and departments to participate.
  4. EngageShare progress updates, leaderboards, reminders, and team milestones.
  5. ReviewReport agreed participation and activity measures, recognise achievements, and recommend next steps.
