Process

How Our Wellness Programmes Work

Every programme moves through five clear stages, so HR teams know what happens when and employees know exactly how to take part.

  1. DiscoverDiscuss workforce size, objectives, work patterns, current activities, and practical constraints.
  2. DesignAgree the challenge format, duration, participation rules, measurement approach, and recognition plan.
  3. LaunchProvide clear communications and encourage employees and departments to participate.
  4. EngageShare progress updates, leaderboards, reminders, and team milestones.
  5. ReviewReport agreed participation and activity measures, recognise achievements, and recommend next steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are corporate wellness programmes, and how can they benefit employees?

Corporate wellness programmes are organised activities that help employees move more and build healthier habits, such as step challenges, walking and running campaigns and group fitness sessions. They encourage regular activity, give colleagues a shared goal and make wellness part of the working week.

What types of corporate wellness activities do you offer in Ahmedabad?

Corporate step challenges, walking and running challenges, department-versus-department competitions, monthly themed wellness challenges, and guided group fitness and running sessions. Activities can run as one-off campaigns or as a structured annual wellness calendar.

Can corporate wellness programmes be customised according to company size and employee fitness levels?

Yes. Each programme is designed around your workforce size, departments, work patterns, locations and the fitness levels of your employees, with options that welcome beginners as well as regular runners.

Do you organise corporate step challenges, walking competitions and group fitness sessions?

Yes. Step challenges, walking and running competitions and group fitness sessions are core parts of the programme. Session design depends on your location, facilities, schedule and participant needs.

How can corporate wellness programmes improve employee engagement and teamwork?

Team goals, leaderboards and department challenges give colleagues a reason to connect outside their usual work, celebrate shared progress and build a more positive workplace culture.
