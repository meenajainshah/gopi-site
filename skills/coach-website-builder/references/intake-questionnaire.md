# Intake questionnaire

Read anything the owner already shared first (profile PDF, content doc, Drive folder, social links). Ask only for gaps, in one short message. Record answers in a one-page brief.

## The person and the business
- Name as it should appear, short name, role, city or area served, online or in person, languages.
- Credentials and certifications, with the issuing body. Years practising. Previous career if it helps trust.
- Community roles, memberships, events or organisations they are known for (only what they can stand behind).
- One credential for the share card.

## Services and prices
- Each service: who it is for, what is included, how it is delivered, session length, group or one to one.
- Prices and commitment terms exactly as they want them shown. Refund, pause and cancellation terms (or none yet).
- Capacity (how many clients they can take). Only publish if they want it shown.
- Anything they sell to organisations (companies, schools, event organisers) and how scope is agreed.

## Proof
- Results and client stories they can show, and whether each person agreed to be named. Default: do not name anyone.
- Posts, photos, videos, press, event involvement. Who gave permission.
- Numbers they can back (assignments, years, members). Ask what evidence exists if a number sounds large.

## Goals and audience
- The one thing they most want to be known for in the next six months.
- Ideal client, and the first question that client types into Google.
- What counts as a win: enquiries, bookings, a free download, WhatsApp messages. Target number and by when.
- Competitors or sites they like and dislike.

## Brand
- Logo (or none), colours, fonts, photos they own. A square photo for cards.
- Tone: formal, warm, direct. Words they love and words they never use.

## Technical
- Domain name and where it is registered. Is `www` or the bare domain the main address.
- Hosting they already use (Render, Vercel, Wix, other). Who controls DNS.
- Email address that should receive enquiries. WhatsApp number with country code.
- Social profile links. Google Business Profile status. Analytics already installed.
- Any pages that must exist (free plan, events, blog).

## Confirm before building
List what you will not publish until confirmed. Examples: client names, unverified numbers, legal choices, anything a document says to confirm first.
