# Claims and compliance

## Never publish without confirmation
Client names and stories, results and testimonials, numbers (clients, members, assignments), awards, rankings, company case studies with figures, photos of identifiable people, event involvement the owner has not confirmed. Put each on the confirmation list and ask. A document that says "confirm before publishing" is an instruction.

## Wording
- Say "experience includes" and "involvement with" for roles you only know loosely.
- Present case figures as reported outcomes, never as guaranteed results or proof of cause.
- Do not promise outcomes (times, weight, income, health).

## Health, fitness and nutrition
- Not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting or changing a programme, especially with a condition, an injury, pregnancy or long inactivity.
- Stop and seek advice with pain, dizziness, breathlessness or unusual symptoms.
- Where a condition or therapeutic diet is involved, work with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian.
- A free self-guided plan is general guidance, not an individual assessment. Put the note next to the plan.

## Events and consulting
State that scope, role, authority and fees are agreed in writing. Say what remains the responsibility of other providers (timing, insurance, permissions, safety).

## Privacy
Tell visitors what forms collect and who else sees a visit (messaging apps, embeds, fonts, host). Say honestly whether analytics or advertising cookies are used. Offer a way to ask for access or deletion. Mention the data-protection law that applies where the owner works, and flag it for a lawyer to check.

## Drafts, not legal advice
Privacy and Terms are drafts written from what the site does. Always list the choices the owner must confirm: governing law and courts, refund and cancellation terms, the contact route, and the data-protection wording. Recommend a lawyer review before relying on them.

## Third-party content
Embedded posts and links belong to others. Get the owner's approval for each post, and check it is public (a private post shows as unavailable).
