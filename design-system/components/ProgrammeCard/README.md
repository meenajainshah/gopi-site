A card for one coaching level or programme: a tag, a title, a short paragraph and a goal line.

Provide the chip (`Beginner`, `Intermediate`, `Experienced & Advanced`), a title in uppercase display type, one or two sentences and a goal in mono. Add `gd-card--main` to give the one recommended card an accent border.

- Lay cards out in a grid that fills each row (three across on desktop, one on phones). Do not leave a card alone in a row.
- A footer line (`gd-len`) holds a duration or a link such as the free plan.
