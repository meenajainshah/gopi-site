The hero pace bib: an ink header strip, a big target time, paces per kilometre and mile, and four distance tabs.

Provide the distance label, the finish time, the pace per km and per mile, and which tab is selected (`aria-pressed`). The tabs are Beginner, 10 km, Half and Full; the selected tab takes the `accent` fill.

- Times and paces come from the pacing record only (70 minutes for 10 km at 7:00 per km; 2:30 for the half at about 7:07 per km; under 5 hours for the full).
- The bib has a `border-strong` ink edge and no shadow.
