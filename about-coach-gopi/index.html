About

About Coach GopiBuilding healthier people, stronger communities and better events.

I am Gopi D, a UESCA Certified Running Coach and Certified Nutrition & Diet Coach, based in Ahmedabad.

I have been training runners since 2016. After more than 20 years in IT sales, I transitioned out of my full-time corporate career to focus on running coaching, nutrition, wellness, and running-event support.

My work brings together four areas: helping individuals become stronger runners, supporting healthier everyday habits through nutrition, helping organisations build active workplace cultures, and supporting the planning and delivery of running events.

Running and coaching journey

I coach runners preparing for 10K races, Half Marathons, Full Marathons, high-altitude events, and ultra-distance challenges. My approach combines personalised training plans, training-data review, running-form feedback, strength and mobility guidance, race preparation, and ongoing support.

My pacing experience includes 20 Half Marathon assignments at a 2:30 pace, six 10K assignments at a 70-minute pace, and a sub-5-hour Full Marathon pacing assignment at Vadodara International Marathon in January 2020.

Community

Riverfront Runners Group: community, mentorship and movement

A significant part of my journey is my role as a mentor with Riverfront Runners Group (RRG), an Ahmedabad-based community of more than 200 members. RRG is built around the belief that fitness is more enjoyable and sustainable when people support one another.

RRG activities extend beyond running and include morning jogs, long-distance runs, race training, group runs, cycling, road and trail rides, boot camp activities focused on strength, agility, conditioning and injury prevention, and swimming training guidance.

Run Together. Train Together. Grow Together. Stay Together.

Leadership

Leadership with ADR

I also served as President of ADR (July 2022 to June 2023). During my association, I started a chapter, ADR CK, during 2016 and grew it into the organisation's largest group. I trained and mentored many runners through this community experience, developing a practical understanding of how to encourage participation, build belonging, and help people progress together.

Corporate

Corporate experience and workplace wellness

Before moving into full-time coaching, I spent more than 20 years in IT sales, including leadership responsibilities. That experience helps me understand business environments, team dynamics, employee engagement, and the realities of busy working lives.

I now apply that understanding to corporate wellness programmes, including group step challenges, walking and running campaigns, fitness activities, and department-level competitions. See corporate wellness programmes.

Events

Running event involvement

My event-related experience includes involvement with ADR Night Ultra 4.0, Shantigram ADR 10 km Time Trial, RRG promo runs associated with AU Jaipur Marathon and Udaipur Marathon, and the Mehsana Marathon promo run.
