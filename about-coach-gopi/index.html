My journey

From Corporate Experience to a Passion for Running & Coaching

For more than 20 years I worked in IT sales, including leadership roles. I began training runners in 2016, alongside that career, helping people start running, stay consistent and prepare for their races.

Over time I transitioned out of my full-time corporate career to focus on running coaching, nutrition, workplace wellness and running-event support, bringing the planning, communication and team skills from corporate life into coaching.

Today I coach runners preparing for 10K races, Half Marathons, Full Marathons, high-altitude events and ultra-distance challenges. My marathon coaching and marathon training combine personalised training plans, training-data review, running-form feedback, strength and mobility guidance, race preparation and ongoing support. As a certified running coach, I keep every plan matched to the runner's experience, current fitness and race timeline.

Community

Riverfront Runners Group: Building a Stronger Running Community

A significant part of my journey is my role as a running mentor in Ahmedabad with Riverfront Runners Group Ahmedabad (RRG), a running community in Ahmedabad of more than 200 members. RRG is built around the belief that fitness is more enjoyable and sustainable when people support one another.

Mentoring runners at Riverfront Runners Group

As a mentor, I help new members settle into regular training, guide runners preparing for races, and support the group's long runs and race-training blocks. Many runners join knowing nobody; the running community in Ahmedabad that RRG has built gives them training partners, accountability and people to celebrate their first 10K or marathon with.

RRG activities extend beyond running and include morning jogs, long-distance runs, race training, group runs, cycling, road and trail rides, boot camp activities focused on strength, agility, conditioning and injury prevention, and swimming training guidance.

Run Together. Train Together. Grow Together. Stay Together.

Leadership

Leadership & Community Building with ADR

I served as President of ADR from July 2022 to June 2023. Earlier, in 2016, I started a chapter, ADR CK, and grew it into the organisation's largest group. Through that work I trained and mentored many runners and learned what makes a running group thrive: making beginners feel welcome, giving experienced runners goals to chase, and creating a sense of belonging that keeps people coming back.

That community-building experience shapes everything I do today, from group sessions at the Riverfront to the corporate wellness programmes and running events I help organise.
