Timing-team coordination

Coordinate with the appointed timing provider or timing team to support accurate, well-organised timing operations and a smooth participant experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a running event management consultant do?

A running event management consultant helps organisers plan and deliver a race: event coordination, technical guidance on format and course, timing-team coordination, promotion and participant engagement, and race-day operations, within an agreed scope.

How can a race director help organise a successful running event?

A race director provides event leadership and race-day oversight: briefing the teams, monitoring key milestones, coordinating organisers, volunteers, timing teams and service providers, and making operational decisions within the agreed authority.

Do you provide marathon and half-marathon event management services?

Yes. Support is available for road races including marathons and half marathons, as a technical consultant, race director or coordination support, depending on the event's requirements and agreed scope.

Can you help with race planning and race-day coordination?

Yes. Planning support covers event timelines, operational checklists, start and finish-area planning, race flow and readiness reviews. On race day, support covers briefings, milestone monitoring and coordination between teams.

Do you provide technical consulting for 5K and 10K running events?

Yes. Community 5K and 10K runs, timed trials and promotional runs can all receive technical input on format, race categories, course planning considerations and participant experience.

Can you support corporate runs and community running events?

Yes. Corporate teams, running clubs, schools, colleges and brands planning employee runs, community events or promotional runs can engage support for planning, coordination and race day.
