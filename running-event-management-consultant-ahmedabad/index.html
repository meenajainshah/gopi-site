Events & race management

Running Event Management, Technical Consulting & Race DirectionWell-planned events create memorable experiences.

For runners, organisers, sponsors, and communities. With experience in running communities, race pacing, event coordination, and promotional runs, I can support running-event organisers as a Technical Consultant or Race Director, depending on the event's requirements and agreed scope.

Whether you are planning a community 10K, a timed trial, a marathon promotional run, a night ultra, or a larger road-running event, I can help bring structure to planning, coordination, technical preparation, event communication, and race-day execution.

Event services

1

Event coordination

Support the planning and coordination of event activities, timelines, people, and operational requirements. Help keep organisers, volunteers, vendors, venue teams, and other stakeholders aligned.

  • Pre-event planning and coordination meetings.
  • Event timelines, task tracking, and operational checklists.
  • Coordination with volunteers, vendors, venue and support teams.
  • Participant-flow and race-day activity coordination.
2

Technical guidance and race planning

Practical technical guidance for the running-event format and participant experience, aligned with the organiser's objectives, course conditions, event scale, and applicable requirements.

  • Input on event format, race categories, and participant experience.
  • Course and route-planning considerations with the organiser and relevant authorities.
  • Start and finish-area planning, race flow, and key operational touchpoints.
  • Race-day readiness reviews and issue-escalation planning.
3

Timing-team coordination

Coordinate with the appointed timing provider or timing team to support accurate, well-organised timing operations and a smooth participant experience.

  • Pre-event coordination with the timing provider.
  • Review of race categories, bib and timing requirements, and participant data handover.
  • Coordination of timing checkpoints and start/finish processes, as applicable.
  • Race-day communication and issue escalation between organisers and the timing team.

Timing equipment, chip systems, official result validation, and timing-provider deliverables remain the responsibility of the appointed specialist provider unless separately contracted.

4

Event promotion and participant engagement

Help build awareness and participation through community networks, running-group outreach, promotional runs, and event communication support.

  • Promotion through running communities and relevant networks.
  • Promo-run planning and coordination.
  • Participant communication and event information support.
  • Community engagement and awareness-building activities.
5

Race-day coordination and race director support

Depending on the agreed role, provide race-day leadership or operational support to help teams work to the event plan, respond to issues, and maintain clear communication.

  • Race-day briefing and coordination with assigned teams.
  • Monitoring key operational milestones.
  • Coordination between organisers, volunteers, timing teams, and other service providers.
  • Issue escalation and operational decision support within the agreed authority.
